# Introducing Volvox.Bot: Our AI-Powered Discord Bot

Today, we're excited to officially introduce **Volvox.Bot** — our fully hosted AI-powered Discord bot built to make community management easier, smarter, and completely hassle-free.

Whether you're running a small community, an open-source project, or a large Discord server, Volvox.Bot helps you manage everything from one modern dashboard. Best of all, **it's completely free to get started.**

---

## Why We Built Volvox.Bot

Managing a Discord server often means juggling multiple **Discord bots**, hosting them yourself, keeping them online, and constantly updating configurations.

We wanted to simplify that experience.

Volvox.Bot removes the complexity by giving communities one platform that combines AI, moderation, analytics, support tools, and engagement features — without requiring you to host anything yourself.

Simply add the bot to your server, sign in with Discord, and you're ready to go.

---

## How to Add Volvox.Bot to Your Discord Server

Adding Volvox.Bot takes just a few minutes.

1. Visit **https://volvox.bot**
2. Click **Add to Server**
3. Sign in with your Discord account
4. Select the server you want to add the bot to
5. Configure your server through the web dashboard

No hosting.
No maintenance.
No infrastructure to manage.

We handle everything so you can focus on your community.

> You'll need **Manage Server** permission to add a bot to a Discord server. If you're the owner, you already have it.

## Everything Your Community Needs

Volvox.Bot brings together the tools communities use every day into one easy-to-manage platform. Here's what this **Discord bot** can do for your server.

### 🤖 AI Chat

Give your community an AI assistant that can answer questions, remember conversations, and help members in real time. Mention the bot in any channel and it responds — contextually and intelligently, without needing a command.

### 🧠 User Memory

Volvox.Bot builds long-term memory for each user — remembering preferences, past context, and communication styles over time. Most **Discord bots** forget everything between sessions. Volvox.Bot doesn't.

It's one of the most meaningful ways AI changes what a **Discord bot** can actually do for a community.

### 🛡️ AI Moderation

Automatically detect spam, harmful content, and rule violations while keeping moderators in control. Unlike static keyword filters used by most **Discord server bots**, Volvox.Bot uses model-selectable AI safety scoring with configurable thresholds and actions.

The AI handles volume. Your moderators handle judgment calls.

### 👋 Welcome Messages

Set up dynamic join messages, returning-member greetings, rules verification, and introduction prompts — so every new member gets a proper welcome. First impressions matter, and this **Discord bot** makes them easy to get right.

### 📊 Analytics Dashboard

Track community activity, member engagement, AI usage, and server growth from a single dashboard. No separate analytics **bot for Discord** needed — it's all built in.

### 🎫 Support Tickets

Create organised support workflows with private tickets, status tracking, and transcripts. Perfect for gaming communities, open-source projects, and anyone running a busy server.

### 🏆 Community XP & Leaderboards

Reward active members with XP, levels, reputation, badges, and leaderboards that encourage participation. Keep your community active and give your most engaged members something to work towards.

### 📝 Audit Logs

Keep track of moderation actions, configuration changes, and important server events with detailed logs. Everything is searchable, so nothing gets lost.

### 📄 TL;DR Summaries

Missed a conversation? Generate AI summaries that highlight key topics, decisions, action items, and shared links.

This is a feature you won't find in most standard **bots for Discord** — and one our community has already told us they can't live without.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Volvox.Bot free?**
Yes — completely free to get started. All core features are available without a paid plan.

**Do I need to host it myself?**
No. Volvox.Bot is fully hosted and managed by us. It runs 24/7 without you touching a server.

**How is it different from MEE6?**
MEE6 covers leveling and basic moderation well, but AI chat, analytics, and advanced features require a paid plan. Volvox.Bot includes all of that — plus user memory and TL;DR summaries — for free.

**How is it different from Dyno?**
Dyno is a strong moderation bot but has no AI capabilities. Volvox.Bot gives you everything Dyno does for moderation, plus AI chat, user memory, conversation summaries, and a full analytics dashboard.

**Can I add it to multiple servers?**
Yes. Each server gets its own independent configuration through the dashboard.

**Where can I get help?**
Join our [Discord community](https://discord.gg/8ahXACdamN) or visit the [documentation](https://docs.volvox.bot).
